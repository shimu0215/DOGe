For two eligible tokens, temperature contraction z_i -> m+beta(z_i-m) gives log(q_i/q_j)=beta(z_i-z_j). Thus beta directly contracts every eligible log-odds difference, including high-probability tokens. In contrast, uniform mixing gives q_i=alpha*p_i+c, where alpha=1-epsilon and c=epsilon*M/K. When alpha*p_i and alpha*p_j greatly exceed c, q_i/q_j approximately equals p_i/p_j: much head-token discrimination survives even with a large epsilon. The scalar map log(alpha*p+c), holding alpha,c fixed, has slope alpha*p/(alpha*p+c) with respect to log(p), approaching1 on head tokens. This is an algebraic motivation, not proof that it caused the observed OPD differences.
