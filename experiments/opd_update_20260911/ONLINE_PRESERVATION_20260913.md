# Preservation on online teacher trajectories

The previous entropy4 PCGrad run reached student42.0% on the reused main400 but46.4% on the complete1000. Its rawT1 teacher128 fell from93.75% to86.71875%. Increasing trainable capacity to last4 kept the ordinary main screens but raw128 fell23.4375pp. These observations motivate better coverage of teacher-generated prefixes, not a claim that raw sampling is intrinsically incompatible with suppression.

For the four existing teacher rollouts h_j on a training question, the new online term is lambda/G sum_j mean_t KL(p_0(.|h_j,t) || p_theta(.|h_j,t)), G=4. Previously it was lambda/4 times the sampled-position KL on h_0 alone. We retain the same rawT1 teacher generation, correctness reward, offline anchors, answer supervision, interference schedule and symmetric PCGrad. Thus online preservation has four times the total coefficient and averages all4 existing trajectories instead of one. This is a targeted coverage-and-strength change, not a pure variance-only ablation; it does not add student signals or runtime components.

Conditioned on a frozen sampling policy, averaging independently sampled trajectories estimates the corresponding occupancy-weighted conditional KL. We detach sampled trajectories and omit derivatives of their sampling distribution: this is a semi-gradient update, not the exact derivative of an expectation under the changing model. Nor is this forward conditional KL under current-teacher prefixes the usual sequence-level forward KL under original-teacher occupancy. Consequently we do not invoke Pinsker or the chain rule to promise task accuracy. Actual greedy, ordinary and rawT1 checks plus independent student OPD remain necessary. The finite training group and process-position sampling can miss important prefixes.
