# Bounded pair log-odds and frozen target-fit diagnostic

The new teacher objective changes only the desired pair log-odds in the failed batch4 pair-gap recipe. If g0 is the original teacher log p(a)-log p(b), use g* = -sign(g0)*min(abs(g0),0.5), and retain beta1 Huber loss on g_theta-g*. At the ideal target, the favored candidate in the pair has odds at most exp(0.5) relative to the other. This bounds the desired gap, not actual model logits, entropy, pair probability mass, or teacher quality. It does not preserve the original probability multiset. Large initial errors still have saturated Huber gradients, so early updates may be similar to the uncapped target until closer to the ranking boundary; improvement is uncertain.

Position selection still uses original teacher full-swap divergence, and the four offline question losses are averaged. All other last-layer/anti1.5/anchor12/answer1/ownRL2/64step settings stay fixed. Both loaded models start from original7B; no student parameters, gradients, logits, reward, or simulated updates enter teacher training. The helper only constructs training losses, never inference. CPU gradient/zero-at-clipped-target/common-shift/identity checks and real2update/export checks passed before formal training.

The short-allocation diagnostic reads frozen original7B plus four existing teacher checkpoints: gap, failed gapbatch4, last2 tail, and Top2 head. It measures target KL improvement, native KL, argmax change, entropy change, and observed-token logp change on offline contexts, recording target tokens. No training or student rollout takes place. These are descriptive fit metrics, not a source classifier, deployed gate, causal efficacy ablation, or proof of unknown-student generalization.

Sampling is deliberately a common reference cohort:32 questions exposed to the reference last2 anti objective and32 heldout questions, with available student/teacher/teacher_greedy process contexts. The row split name trained refers only to that REFERENCE negative-question cohort. The failed batch4 teacher did NOT receive those32 as its negative questions, whereas the other three did. Its original training data and step schedule differ. The sidecar teacher_target_fit_9916479_exposure.json records candidate-specific negative question exposure. Teacher-generated trajectories on a training question need not match the exact anchored trajectory. Use the sidecar in every interpretation and do not report a naive candidate train-versus-test gap from the row label alone.

Questions/contexts can be filtered for insufficient process length; group row counts are recorded. Top2 and tail diagnostics may select different token positions. Prefix tokens are not independent statistical samples, and aggregated source differences without matching prefixes and exposure do not prove the model identified who generated a context. The diagnostic is meant to identify whether desired score modifications are realized at all and where preservation fails.
